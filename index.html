What it is

One plan for the people you share money with.

Home Free is for couples, families and housemates paying down debt and saving together. Everyone connects their own accounts, the crew agrees on one plan, and each month the app shows what to pay, who's done their part and when you'll be debt-free.

A debt-free date

Pick highest-interest first or smallest balance first, set what each of you puts in, and see the month you'll be free.

Your crew, together

Invite your partner or family. Everyone sees the shared debts, this month's payments and who's paid what.

Every month, on track

What's due and when, marked paid in a tap, with due dates in your calendar if you want them.

Then, save

Add a goal, like an emergency fund. It fills once the debt is gone, or alongside it if you'd rather.

How it works

Three steps, then just check in.

  1. Connect your banks

    Each person links their own cards and loans through Plaid. Balances, rates and due dates come in on their own, or add a debt by hand.

  2. Make the plan together

    Choose which debt goes first and how much extra each of you puts in. The plan shows the payments and the debt-free month.

  3. Pay, mark it, watch it shrink

    Home shows what's next and who's paid what. Progress shows the debt going down month by month.

Private by design

Your money is yours. We just help you plan it.

  • We never see your bank password. Banks connect through Plaid.
